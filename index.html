Common questions

Everything parents usually ask before enrolling.

How large are the batches? →

Each batch has 3–4 students maximum. This ensures every student gets personal attention, and no one hesitates to ask questions.

What are the timings? →

We offer weekday evening slots (after school hours) and weekend morning/afternoon sessions. Timings are flexible and arranged based on the batch.

Is there a trial class? →

Yes — every new student gets a free trial session. No payment, no commitment. Come see if it's a good fit.

Where exactly in Muweilah? →

Building No. 2703, Suni's Kitchen Building, School Zone, Muweilah, Sharjah. We're right in the school cluster area — walkable from Al Zahia, University City, and surrounding residential buildings. Open in Google Maps →

Which curricula do you cover? →

CBSE, ICSE, and UAE MoE curriculum. The teacher is experienced across all three and adapts the approach based on each student's board.
